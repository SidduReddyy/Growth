## 🎯 Growth Plan Highlights

### Target Persona
- **Primary:** 1st & 2nd-year B.Tech / BCA / MCA students in Tier-2 and Tier-3 Indian engineering colleges.
- **Pain Point:** Overwhelmed by generic AI hype and textbook theory; craving a tangible, portfolio-ready project in under 60 minutes with zero prior coding prerequisite.

### 3-Channel Acquisition Engine (Target: 500 Registrations)

```
Total Registrations Target: 500
Budget: ₹5,000 (~$60) | Blend CAC: ₹10/registration
```

1. **WhatsApp & Telegram Student Communities (Organic / Viral Loop — Target: 250 Registrations | ₹0 CAC)**
   - Curated micro-copy shared across 35+ verified college WhatsApp groups.
   - 2-sided referral incentive: *"Refer 2 batchmates, unlock the Exclusive AI Starter Toolkit & Prompt Cheat Sheet."*
   
2. **Campus Ambassador Sprint (Target: 150 Registrations | ₹10 CAC)**
   - Recruited 10 energetic campus leads across target colleges.
   - Performance milestone tiering: Certificate of Excellence + ₹300 stipend upon reaching 15 verified registrations.

3. **Hyper-Targeted Instagram & LinkedIn Micro-Ads (Target: 100 Registrations | ₹35 CAC)**
   - Short-form vertical reels highlighting: *"Stop watching AI tutorials. Build your own working AI project in 60 mins tonight."*
   - Strict geofencing and interest targeting on Tier-2/3 tech university clusters.

- **Stack:** Semantic HTML5, Modern Vanilla CSS (Glassmorphism, custom CSS custom properties, responsive grid/flexbox), Vanilla JavaScript (no bloat).
- **Key Conversion Features:**
  - ⚡ **Dynamic Particle Canvas Background** matching NxtWave futuristic branding.
  - ⏳ **Live Countdown Timer** creating urgency and FOMO.
  - 🎟️ **Real-Time Dynamic Seat Counter** ("Only 87 seats left out of 500").

  - ❓ **Interactive FAQ Accordion** addressing trust and beginner-friendly concerns.
  - 🏆 **Social Proof & Testimonials** showcasing past student project outcomes.
