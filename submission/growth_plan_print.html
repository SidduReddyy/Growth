🧪 6. Three Core Growth Tests (Hypothesis → Test → Metric)
Test 1: Headline Value Proposition (Skill vs. Career Outcome)
Hypothesis: Engineering students are motivated more by immediate resume/placement outcomes than abstract AI learning. A hook emphasizing "Add a Working AI Project to Your Resume Tonight" will yield a 35% higher registration conversion than "Learn AI Fundamentals in 60 Minutes".
What to Test: A/B test hero headline across 200 incoming student clicks: Variant A (Skill-focused) vs. Variant B (Placement-outcome focused).
Metric: Visitor-to-registration conversion rate (CVR %).
Test 2: Referral Incentive Structure (Certificate vs. Placement Kit)
Hypothesis: Students are significantly more motivated to share if the unlockable incentive offers tangible placement utility rather than generic participation badges.
What to Test: Test two post-registration referral offers: Variant A ("Refer 3 friends for an Advanced AI Certificate") vs. Variant B ("Refer 3 friends to unlock Top 50 AI Interview Questions & Resume Template").
Metric: Referral Viral K-Factor (% of registrants who click WhatsApp share and generate ≥1 secondary signup).
Test 3: Live Show-Up Optimization (WhatsApp Ping vs. Email Calendar)
Hypothesis: Free webinars typically suffer 20-30% show-up rates. A direct WhatsApp 15-minute ping with a 1-click Google Colab link will push live attendance to 50%+.
What to Test: Group A receives standard email calendar notification; Group B receives WhatsApp ping with "Zero setup needed — Click to join live workshop".
Metric: Live attendance show-up rate (% of registered students attending ≥30 minutes).
