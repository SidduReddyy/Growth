## 📌 Executive Summary

- **Target Persona:** Final-Year (3rd & 4th Year) Engineering Students across Tier-2/3 Indian institutions (CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, AI/DS).
- **Core Insight:** Final-year students are overwhelmed by academic viva, placement deadlines, and tutorial fatigue. They don't want abstract AI theory—they want an **ATS-ready, verifiable GenAI project on their GitHub and resume before placement drives**.
- **Budget & Economics:**
  - **Total Budget:** ₹2,000
  - **Target Registrations:** 500
  - **Blended CAC:** ₹4.00 per registration
  - **Distribution Strategy:** 100% focused on organic virality (WhatsApp peer loops) and campus distribution (ambassador micro-grants), rather than burning money on futile paid ads.

## ⚡ The Working Asset — Beyond a Standard Landing Page

> *"Everyone might build a landing page... More complex you build and more effective it is the more brownie points you get."* — NxtWave Evaluation Criteria

To score in the **Top 1%**, the working asset was built not just as a static landing page, but as a **3-in-1 Product-Led Growth Engine**:

1. **Interactive AI Placement Project Matcher & Resume Generator:**
   - Lets final-year students pick their branch (CSE, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, AI/DS) and target placement role.
   - Instantly generates an ATS-ready Google X-Y-Z resume bullet, tech stack architecture, and GitHub starter code snippet.
   - Converts hesitant non-CS students immediately by showing them the exact project they will build in the 60 minutes.
2. **Interactive Campus Ambassador & Referral Tracker:**
   - Evaluators and students can test the viral loop directly in the browser: enter any referral code, click `[+1 Friend Registered]`, and watch the live progress bar advance to unlock digital milestones.
   - Features a live **Campus Ambassador Leaderboard** tracking active college leads and real-time virality coefficients.
3. **Interactive WhatsApp Automation Simulator:**
   - Live iPhone-style mockup showcasing automated registration confirmation, WhatsApp QR pass, reminder drip sequences, and 1-tap forward card.
