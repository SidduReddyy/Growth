### Channel 1: WhatsApp Viral Loops (250 Registrations)

**Why WhatsApp?** It's where engineering students actually communicate daily. Batch groups, branch groups, hostel groups — every student is in 10+ active groups.

**The Mechanic:**
1. Student registers on our landing page → Gets a unique referral link
2. Share with 3 friends → Unlock "AI Starter Toolkit" (curated project ideas + interview prep)
3. Each friend who registers gets THEIR own referral link → Creates viral chain

**Funnel Math:**
- Seed 50 initial registrations (from ambassador network + personal outreach)
- Average student is in 5 college WhatsApp groups (40-60 members each)
- If 20% of seed users share → 10 shares
- Each share reaches ~200 students (multiple groups)
- 5% conversion on shared links = 100 new registrations from first wave
- Second wave repeats at 60% efficiency → 60 more
- Third wave → 40 more
- **Organic growth from curiosity/FOMO:** 50 more
- **Total: ~250 registrations**

## 📌 Slide 5: Thought Process & AI Reflection

### What Changed Between My First Idea and Final Solution?

**First idea:** Build a standard landing page + run a ₹2,000 Instagram ad campaign.
**Problem:** ₹2,000 on paid ads would get ~5,000-10,000 impressions → ~100-200 clicks → ~30-50 registrations. Nowhere near 500.

**Final insight:** The budget is too small for paid channels alone. The only way to hit 500 is through **organic virality**. So the ₹2,000 should go toward **enabling virality** (ambassador incentives + one Reel boost) rather than direct acquisition.

The landing page itself became the growth tool — with built-in referral mechanics that turn every registrant into a promoter.

### If I Had Another 24 Hours, I Would:
1. **Build a WhatsApp Bot** using Twilio/WhatsApp Business API — automated registration flow, reminder sequences, and referral tracking directly in WhatsApp (no website needed)
2. **Add an Ambassador Dashboard** — leaderboard showing which ambassador got the most registrations, adding competition/gamification
3. **Create an AI-Powered "Will You Get Placed?" Quiz** — viral hook that feeds into workshop registration ("Your AI score is low → join this workshop to fix it")
4. **A/B test the landing page headline** — "Build Your First AI Project" vs "Add AI to Your Resume in 60 Minutes"

### What AI Suggested That I Rejected (and Why):

| What I Asked | What AI Suggested | What I Changed & Why |
|---|---|---|
| "How to get 500 registrations with ₹2,000?" | AI suggested splitting budget across Facebook Ads, Google Ads, and influencer payments | **Rejected** — ₹2,000 spread across paid channels gives negligible reach. Concentrated on WhatsApp virality (₹0 cost) + 1 targeted Reel boost instead. Budget goes to ambassador reimbursements. |
| "Design a landing page for students" | AI suggested a bright, colorful, Canva-style design with stock photos | **Rejected** — Engineering students are savvy. A premium, dark-themed, techy design builds more trust and signals "this is legit, not a scam." Used generated AI art instead of generic stock photos. |
| "What referral incentive should I offer?" | AI suggested cash rewards (₹50 per referral) or Amazon vouchers | **Rejected** — Budget doesn't support cash incentives, and it attracts low-quality registrations (people registering fake accounts). Instead, offered the "AI Starter Toolkit" (digital, zero cost to deliver, genuinely valuable for the target audience). |
